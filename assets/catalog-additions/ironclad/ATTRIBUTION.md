# Ironclad artwork sources

Reviewed September 25, 2026. Underlying game artwork belongs to its respective
rights holders, including Sony Interactive Entertainment and Arrowhead Game
Studios. Source availability and credits are not redistribution permission.

- Cover: unchanged promotional image from [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/09/15/helldivers-2-ironclad-democracy-warbond-launches-sept-22/).
- All eight equipment pictures: named game-derived images from [Helldivers 2 Challenges](https://www.helldivers2challenges.com/tiermaker/). Upstream extraction/editing authorship unverified; not asserted publisher-supplied originals.
- Arbitrator, Evictor, Sai and Breacher are cropped only to remove excess empty padding. Their original transparent backgrounds and all retained pixels are preserved. No background segmentation, AI redrawing, resampling or recoloring.
- The small Surplus EAT icon retains its source resolution rather than invented detail.
