# M2B Warbond batch 4 — source review

Checked September 14, 2026 against the verified v1.1.6 catalog. Scope: acquisition, equipment-set membership, aliases, one subgroup correction and promotional artwork. No damage values, prices, entitlement assumptions or live catalog scraping.

## Masters of Ceremony

[Arrowhead announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/05/08/masters-of-ceremony-warbond-marches-into-helldivers-2-may-15/), Mitchell Ayre, May 8, 2025; release May 15. It supports R-2 Amendment, CQC-2 Sabre, G-142 Pyrotech Grenade and CQC-1 One True Flag. The Saber spelling/identity already has a reviewed record and is not changed again.

The fifth item, Sample Scanner, is supported by the [community item procurement entry](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Sample_Scanner) and [Warbond contents](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Masters_of_Ceremony_Premium_Warbond), not the official article text. Mark it community-source. Direct wiki opens failed; indexed page contents supplied corroboration. Do not claim the official announcement lists the booster.

Keep Amendment's marksman-rifle subgroup; its [community weapon entry](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Amendment) corroborates that classification. Keep One True Flag as support, distinct from the sidearm Saber. Whole-set ownership acts on exactly five IDs, including the previously reviewed Saber.

## Force of Law

[Arrowhead announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/06/05/helldivers-2-force-of-law-warbond-launches-june-12/), Katherine Baskin, June 5, 2025; release June 12. Five supported entries: AR-32 Pacifier, G-109 Urchin, AX/ARC-3 Guard Dog K-9, GL-52 De-Escalator and Stun Pods. Review all five acquisitions as primary-source. The two stratagems move from unassigned to this Warbond. No sidearm is implied.

K-9's backpack subgroup is separately corroborated by community [equipment traits](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Equipment_Traits) and [backpack category](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Backpack_Stratagems), not claimed as verbatim official taxonomy. Preserve its old guard dog arc alias and support-colored rim.

## Dust Devils

[Arrowhead announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/08/26/helldivers-2-into-the-unjust-launches-september-2/), Katherine Baskin, August 26, 2025; Warbond release September 4. It identifies one primary, one throwable and three stratagems: AR-2 Coyote, G-7 Pineapple, S-11 Speargun, EAT-700 Expendable Napalm and MS-11 Solo Silo. All five acquisitions are primary-source; three stratagem associations were unassigned. No booster or sidearm is implied.

Retain support classification, including Solo Silo: its [community entry](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Solo_Silo) describes a supply/support weapon, not an orbital strike. Full designations become aliases; existing short names remain intact.

## Exclusions and preservation

The [community Superstore table](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Superstore) separates P-92 Warrant from Force of Law and CQC-42 Machete from Dust Devils. Preserve their earlier Superstore reviews and stable IDs, including Machete's historical `sidearm:cqc-19-machete`. Masters' themed shop set contains cosmetics/armor, not another rollable member. Artwork alone never establishes acquisition; Hot Dog remains outside these sets awaiting its own review.

Fourteen pending acquisitions become reviewed: **13 primary + 1 community**. Catalog: **69 primary + 13 community + 123 pending = 205 entries**, 82 reviewed. Six stratagem source associations and one backpack subgroup change. No canonical names, IDs, eligibility defaults or item-artwork paths change. Saves, history and custom metadata remain protected.
