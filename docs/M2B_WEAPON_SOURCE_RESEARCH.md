# M2B weapon source research — bounded first pass

Researched September 14, 2026. This is evidence for review, not a claim that the complete legacy catalog has been audited. Scope: the 16 `Unassigned / Custom` primary, sidearm and throwable rows in the M2A baseline, plus ARC-12 Blitzer and StA-11 SMG. No catalog, application, artwork, ownership or personal profile was changed by this research.

## Evidence standard

- **Primary acquisition**: an Arrowhead/PlayStation announcement or official publisher material explicitly connects the item to its acquisition source.
- **Community acquisition**: the current Helldivers Wiki procurement entry supports the source, but no primary statement of that specific acquisition route was found in this bounded search. Treat this as corroborated, not primary-verified.
- Official patch notes establish identities and equipment categories but do **not** establish how an item is acquired unless they actually say so. The Arrowhead support archive sometimes displays a recent page-update date; that is not the original patch release date.

Of the 16 catch-all rows, the proposed sources are: 3 starter items, 1 Liberty Day gift, 1 edition entitlement, 5 Warbond unlocks, and 6 separate Superstore purchases. The two additional rows already have the appropriate Warbond association; their categories need correction.

## Implementation constraints for the next bounded change

1. Preserve all 18 stable IDs, existing player ownership/enabled flags, custom metadata and historical scoring. Metadata corrections do not imply that a player acquired anything new.
2. Add the exact old display labels **CQC-1 Saber**, **CQC-19 Machete** and **CQC-2 Stun Lance** to their respective aliases before changing canonical names. Also accept CQC-2 Sabre as an orthographic alias for Saber. Avoid numeric-only CQC aliases: the existing wrong numbers overlap legitimate different weapons.
3. Model the six shop items as **Superstore purchases**, separately from optional theme labels. A bulk “own Warbond” action must not grant Double Freedom, Warrant, Machete, Stun Baton, Combat Hatchet or Smokescreen. Their price/rotation is not needed for the app and is not guaranteed by this audit.
4. Model the Knight as an edition entitlement, the three default items as starter equipment, and Constitution as a gift/event source rather than inventing a Warbond relationship.
5. StA-11 has two acquisition histories. The official December 2025 announcement preserves previously gifted items but does not give every gift-only recipient the whole new Warbond. An owned StA-11 must not automatically mark Righteous Revenants wholly owned, and changing its source must not erase legacy ownership.
6. The current app has `energy`, `smg` and `special-sidearm`, but no existing **Special primary** subgroup. Add a deliberate `special-primary` group with filter/render tests when fixing Torcher, Dominator and Variable; do not silently mislabel them or broaden this task into a complete category refactor. This is an implementation proposal, not a current catalog enum.
7. Retain local artwork and legacy artwork aliases. This research did not download, replace or license any images. A corrected weapon name must still resolve its existing local image.

## Remaining uncertainty and audit boundary

- The ten **Community acquisition** rows (three starters, Constitution and six shop items) still need item-specific primary evidence or owner in-game confirmation if the project requires every acquisition to be primary-verified. Their current wiki records are stronger than the old catch-all labels but should not be represented as official API data.
- Torcher and Variable have primary confirmation of their identity, primary slot and Warbond, while the exact current **Special** menu subgroup is community-corroborated. Dominator and Blitzer have direct official category-change notes.
- No account ownership API was queried. This app remains a locally curated companion, not a proof of a user's game entitlements.
- This pass does not audit the other legacy weapons, boosters, 86 catch-all stratagems, the full Warbond catalog, warbond cover provenance, or mission/planet availability. It does not complete M2B.
