## Viper Commandos — four rollable items

Existing K-2 designation is retained; the prose does not quote its code. Armor, capes, emotes and vehicle skins are not rollable equipment. Its fictional armor story mentioning Orbital Laser does not establish a Warbond stratagem. The unmodified official jungle promotional scene is bundled as the card image, not described as an exact in-game cover.

## Truth Enforcers — four rollable items

[Official PlayStation announcement, October 24, 2024](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/10/24/helldivers-2-truth-enforcers-warbond-launches-oct-31/) gives an October 31 release. The article's unmodified titled promotional artwork is bundled locally. Canonical spellings and weapon slots already match; no names or subgroups change.

The Dominator already has primary-source acquisition evidence and remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Breaker Incendiary gains primary-source acquisition evidence. The announcement's generic game OG image was rejected; its captioned Dominator scene is used instead, explicitly labeled promotional rather than an exact in-game cover. Historical edition bundles are not treated as proof of player ownership today.
