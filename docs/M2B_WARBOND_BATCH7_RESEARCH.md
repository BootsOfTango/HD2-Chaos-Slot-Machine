## Entrenched Division — six items, March 17, 2026

[Arrowhead's March 10 announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/03/10/helldivers-2-entrenched-division-premium-warbond-out-march-17/) supports Stoker, Veto, Entrenchment Tool, Giga Grenade, Gas Mortar and Cremator. Their official full designations are added as aliases, preserving short-name save compatibility. Stoker is explicitly a submachine gun, correcting its old shotgun subgroup. Cremator remains a support weapon despite its fuel backpack; the backpack does not become an extra equipment grant. No booster or cosmetics enter the roll pool.

Sweeper is separate: the [community wiki procurement entry](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Sweeper) places SG-97 Sweeper on the themed Superstore page and calls it a primary shotgun; [the second community wiki](https://helldivers.fandom.com/wiki/SG-97_Sweeper) corroborates both facts. Direct article readers were blocked/unavailable; indexed page excerpts supplied the procurement and category evidence. This is explicitly **community-source**, not official verification. Move it out of the Warbond into Superstore, correct marksman-rifle to shotgun and add the SG-97 alias. Do not promise price or current store rotation.
