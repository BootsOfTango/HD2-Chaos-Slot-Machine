# Defensive acquisition review — September 15, 2026

Scope: fourteen existing defensive stratagems. Each individual community Wiki page was read using the public MediaWiki parse API with redirects enabled; original responses are retained under `.test-data/defensive-research`. Each correction in `assets/catalog-reviews/2026-09-15-defensive.json` points to the canonical page. These are **community-source acquisition facts**, not primary Arrowhead confirmation or an account entitlement check.

All fourteen item infoboxes explicitly list a Requisition Slips unlock price: Anti-Personnel Minefield, Anti-Tank Mines, Autocannon Sentry, EMS Mortar Sentry, Gas Mines, Gatling Sentry, Grenadier Battlement, HMG Emplacement, Incendiary Mines, Machine Gun Sentry, Mortar Sentry, Rocket Sentry, Shield Generator Relay and Tesla Tower. In-game level requirements still apply; ownership and inclusion remain the player's choice in HD2CSM. No prices, balance values or level requirements are added to gameplay logic in this pass.

Important distinctions:

- [Gas Mines](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/MD-8_Gas_Mines) and [Grenadier Battlement](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/E/GL-21_Grenadier_Battlement) list current requisition procurement. Their historical Major Order availability does not make them participant-only campaign rewards now.
- [Machine Gun Sentry](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/A/MG-43_Machine_Gun_Sentry) lists a requisition price even though its level requirement is one. Level-one availability is not a free starter grant.
- EMS Mortar Sentry remains its own defensive item, distinct from Orbital EMS Strike and its retired `EMS Strike` alias. No extra roll entries were introduced.
- The four previously reviewed Warbond defensive items (Flame Sentry, Gas Mortar Sentry, Laser Sentry and Anti-Tank Emplacement) remain untouched, as does campaign-reward Eagle Gas Airstrike.

## Bounded category check

The [Stratagems overview](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stratagems) separates defensive equipment into sentries and emplacements. Public categorymembers page-only responses list ten Sentry Stratagems and nine Emplacement Stratagems pages, with no continuation. The latter includes the explicitly named `April Fools/Budget Helldiver` page, not ordinary selectable equipment. The other eighteen entries match the eighteen existing defensive catalog items (four previously reviewed Warbond items plus this fourteen-item batch). No missing ordinary selectable defensive entry was found in these sources. The generic Defensive Stratagems category returned no page members and was not treated as evidence of an empty equipment list.

This is dated community-category parity, not proof of complete game-wide coverage. The 27 remaining support/backpack/vehicle acquisitions and broader missing-content audit remain queued. Current source facts are reviewed release data, not silently scraped into a player's installed app.
