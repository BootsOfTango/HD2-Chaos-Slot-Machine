# M2B batch 6 — Python Commandos, Redacted Regiment, Siege Breakers

Reviewed September 14, 2026. Fifteen existing pending acquisitions verified with primary-source announcements, seven stratagem associations corrected, two backpack subgroups corrected and eight full-designation aliases added. M2 is not complete.

## Python Commandos — four items, December 2, 2025

[Official November 25 announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/11/25/helldivers-2-python-commandos-premium-warbond-airdrops-dec-2/) specifies AR/GL-21 One-Two and three stratagems: M-1000 Maxigun, AX/FLAM-75 “Guard Dog” Hot Dog and CQC-9 Defoliation Tool. Existing short names remain canonical; full designations become aliases. The article describes Hot Dog returning to its backpack, supporting the backpack subgroup correction. No sidearm or booster is included. Machete references are comparison text, not additional equipment grants. Publisher HTML was retrieved directly when the web reader timed out. Its original jungle/minigun promotional scene is bundled unchanged.

## Redacted Regiment — six items, January 22, 2026

[Original January 13 announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/01/13/helldivers-2-redacted-regiment-warbond-launches-jan-20/) identifies AR-59 Suppressor, R-72 Censor, P-35 Re-Educator, B/MD C4 Pack, TM-01 Lure Mine and Concealed Insertion. C4 is explicitly a backpack. TM-01 is accepted as an alias while keeping the existing TM-1 identity; [the community item reference](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/TM-1_Lure_Mine) corroborates that current spelling. The article's January 20 date was superseded: [official release-day patch 5.0.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/553850/view/509603414731129453?l=english), January 22, says the Warbond activates that day at 14:00 CET. The equipment acquisition evidence remains primary-source. Armor/capes/emotes and separate shop items are excluded.

## Siege Breakers — five items, February 3, 2026

[Official January 27 announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/01/27/helldivers-2-siege-breakers-premium-warbond-drops-february-3/) identifies Trident, CQC-20 Breaching Hammer, GL-28 Belt-Fed Grenade Launcher, EAT-411 Leveller and G/SH-39 Shield. The Shield remains a throwable, not a backpack stratagem. Existing abbreviated stratagem names gain full-name aliases. No booster, sidearm or tank is granted by this Warbond; cosmetics and armor are excluded. The article calls Trident LAS-16, but [Arrowhead's current Known Issues page](https://arrowhead.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15916898652700--HELLDIVERS-2-Known-Issues) uses LAS-13 Trident. Preserve LAS-13 rather than importing the conflicting number as a new identity or an unsupported current name.

## Images, compatibility and limits

Three original publisher promotional scenes are used as card images, not represented as exact in-game covers. Redacted Regiment and Siege Breakers OG URLs contain a 2028 CDN directory although their articles are dated 2026; preserve the observed URLs, do not infer release dates from storage paths. Original dimensions: 2560×1440, 2560×1440 and 2047×1123. No cropping, recoloring, upscaling or AI substitution. Per-image URLs, SHA-256, measured sizes and visual observations are in assets/warbonds/official/provenance.json; game imagery remains its owners' property, and attribution is not a redistribution license.

The v1.1.8 baseline was independently compared to its accepted ASAR before edits. The test fixture protects all 190 unreviewed items, prior 16 Warbond groups, IDs/defaults/art and historical Results. Ownership remains independent of source facts; no automatic grants. The existing “detonation tool” compatibility alias remains recoverable. These facts are reviewed/release-based, not silently scraped into player installs. Planet refresh/factions, richer missions, the map and full Armory redesign are outside this batch.
